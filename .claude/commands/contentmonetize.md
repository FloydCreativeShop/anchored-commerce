# /contentmonetize

Make the user's existing content earn money. Find what already gets attention, connect each piece to a product, fix the path from post to purchase, and repurpose winners across channels. Use no paid ads.

Focus from the user (may be empty): $ARGUMENTS

## Steps

1. **Audit the content.** Use whichever connectors are available, and note any that aren't connected:
   - **Metricool**: posts, pins, reels and videos from the last 90 days on each network, with reach and impressions, saves, shares, comments, and link or outbound clicks. Also get the best times to post.
   - **Shopify**: sessions and orders by social referrer, and the top landing pages from social.
   - **Gumroad**: views and sales by referrer.

   List the **top 10 pieces of content** by saves plus shares plus clicks, with links where available.
2. **Diagnose where money leaks.** For each top piece, check:
   - Does it link to a product, and is the product live and published?
   - Does the link land on the right page (the product, not the homepage)?
   - Is there a clear call to action?
   - Does the product match the promise of the post?

   Call out the biggest leaks with numbers, for example "20k Pinterest impressions a day, 1 store order", or "planner posts point to unpublished Gumroad products".
3. **Make a match for each top piece.** Give it:
   - **Product**: the existing product to attach, or a new one to create, with its price.
   - **New CTA**: a rewritten call to action, under 15 words, and one comment to pin.
   - **Landing**: the landing page to use, and one fix to make on it.
   - **Lead magnet**: a free item to swap in where a sale is too big an ask, to collect emails.
4. **Plan the repurposing.** Turn the top 3 pieces into new content for each channel:
   - **Pinterest**: 3 pin variations with title and description written for search, and the boards to post them to.
   - **Instagram**: 1 carousel outline and 1 Reel script with a hook in the first 3 seconds.
   - **TikTok**: 1 video script.
   - **Facebook**: 1 post.
   - **`/motion`**: 1 prompt for an animated version.
   - **Email**: 1 newsletter snippet.
5. **Add more ways to earn**, each low-effort and with no ads. Suggest only ones that fit the user's brands, and estimate income for each, labeled as an estimate:
   - affiliate links (for example Canva, Etsy tools, wellness products)
   - Pinterest idea pins that link to Gumroad
   - a free-to-paid funnel
   - Instagram broadcast channel drops
   - Gumroad memberships (Monthly Reset Club)
   - digital tip jars
   - UGC or brand-deal pitches
6. **Build a 14-day content-to-cash calendar.** For each day, give the channel, the content (new or repurposed), the product being pushed, the CTA, and the best time to post. Weight it toward the channels that already convert.
7. **Name the 3 moves for this week**, ranked by expected revenue ÷ effort.
8. **Offer next steps.** Wait for an explicit yes before each one:
   - schedule the calendar in Metricool as drafts or for review
   - publish or fix the linked products
   - make the graphics in Canva
   - add content-to-sales tracking to the channels dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/9EJnkcLTmK3NcFtxpm8K8E

## Rules
- Never post, publish, schedule live, change prices or email without the user's explicit approval. Use drafts or review mode.
- Disclose affiliate links in captions (#affiliate or "I earn a small commission").
- Don't invent performance numbers. Every figure is either from a connector or labeled as an estimate.
- Keep the output scannable: tables and short bullets, no filler.
