# Anchored Faith: Posting Guide

**Accounts:** the Anchored Faith Facebook page and Instagram @anchoredfaith11
**Content calendars:** `december-2026-affirmations.md` and `january-2027-affirmations.md` (to read), plus matching `-posts.csv` files (to schedule, with ready-to-paste captions for each platform)

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Sunday | Rest and worship (Advent themes in December) |
| Mon–Wed | Courage, identity, trust |
| Thursday | Storms and the anchor. This is the signature brand theme. |
| Friday | Encouragement for the end of the week |
| Saturday | Hope and the future, with a soft link-in-bio mention |

## Graphic design (free in Canva)

- **Sizes:** 1080×1350 (4:5) for the Instagram feed and Facebook. 1080×1920 for Stories and Reels covers.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#1F2A44`, sea-glass `#A8C3BC`, warm sand `#E9DCC6`, soft gold `#C9A45C`
- **Fonts:** a serif such as Playfair Display for the affirmation, a clean sans-serif such as Montserrat for the verse reference
- **Layout:** the affirmation in large text near the center, the verse reference small underneath, a small anchor icon with "Anchored Faith ⚓" in the bottom corner
- **Make it repeatable:** build 3 templates (light sand, navy, photo with overlay) and rotate them so the feed looks consistent

## When to post (adjust once you have 2 weeks of insights)

- **Instagram:** 7:00–8:00 a.m. for the morning devotional crowd. Add a Story repost at about 8 p.m.
- **Facebook:** 6:30–7:30 a.m., or 12:00 p.m.
- After 2 weeks, check Meta Business Suite insights and move to the best-performing hour.

## How to grow without ads

1. **Instagram:** Ask people to *save* and *share*. The algorithm weights those most. The CSV captions already include this.
2. **Facebook:** End each post with the question to get comments. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
3. Turn 2 affirmations a week into 7-second Reels: the graphic plus soft music plus a slow zoom.
4. Use 1–2 hashtags on Facebook and about 10 on Instagram. The Instagram sets rotate A/B/C, and set C is seasonal (Advent and Christmas in December, new-year themes in January).
5. Share each post to your personal Facebook profile and 1–2 faith groups where self-promotion is allowed.

## Scheduling

- **Free:** Meta Business Suite → Planner → Create post. It schedules both Facebook and Instagram together.
- **Bulk:** upload `december-2026-posts.csv` to a scheduler that supports CSV imports, such as Metricool. Then attach each day's graphic.

> Check every verse against your preferred translation before you publish.
