# /nichefinder

Find niches the user can launch digital products into quickly, sell on Shopify, Gumroad and Etsy, and grow with organic traffic only (Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, SEO). Base the picks on evidence, not hunches.

Seed topic or audience from the user (may be empty): $ARGUMENTS

## Brands and what they already sell
- **Floyd Creative Shop**: https://floydcreativeshop.myshopify.com. It sells affirmation cards, digital wallpapers, journals, printables, and CJdropshipping products.
- **Floyd Essentials**: affirmations. Instagram @floydessentials.
- **Anchored Movement Co**: large-print fitness and wellness planners. Gumroad store anchoredmovementco.gumroad.com, Instagram @anchoredmovementco.
- **Ground rules**: no paid ads, no inventory, a solo creator using AI plus free tools (Canva, Claude).

## Steps

1. **Start from what's already working.** Pull the last 90 days from whichever connectors are available, and note any that aren't connected:
   - **Shopify**: top products, sessions by social referrer, and search terms if available.
   - **Gumroad**: products and their views.
   - **Metricool**: Pinterest impressions, saves and outbound clicks, plus top posts on each network.

   Note which themes and audiences already get attention. Those are the strongest seeds.
2. **Research demand.** Use web search, and check its date so the findings are current. Look for:
   - Etsy and Gumroad best-seller and "popular now" signals for the seed and nearby topics
   - Pinterest Trends and seasonal search patterns for the next 90 days (the holidays, New Year resolutions, Valentine's Day)
   - TikTok and Instagram hashtag momentum
   - Google autocomplete and "people also ask" phrasing

   Cite where each signal came from.
3. **Generate 12 candidate niches.** Make each one specific: an audience plus a problem plus a format. "Large-print daily affirmation cards for seniors with arthritis" is specific; "affirmations" is not. Favor:
   - audiences the user already reaches (fitness, wellness, large-print, faith, women 35+)
   - adjacent micro-niches (caregivers, nurses, teachers, new moms, sobriety, grief, chronic illness, ADHD, retirees)
4. **Score each niche from 1 to 5** on:
   - **Demand**: is it searched, saved, and bought?
   - **Competition**: 5 means few strong listings. Note roughly how many Etsy results there are and how good the top 10 look.
   - **Fit**: does it match the user's existing brands and audience?
   - **Speed**: can it go live in 1 day or less with Canva and Claude?
   - **Price power**: can it carry $7–$27 or a bundle?
   - **Organic reach**: is it Pinterest-friendly and shareable?

   Show a total out of 30.
5. **Show the results.** A ranked table of all 12, then the **top 3** in depth. For each of the top 3, give:
   - **Who exactly buys it**, and the moment they buy it.
   - **3 product ideas** for the niche (a tripwire, a core product, and a bundle), each with a recommended price.
   - **Store**: the best one for it (Shopify, Gumroad or Etsy), with the reason.
   - **10 buyer-intent keywords and long-tail phrases** for titles and tags.
   - **Pinterest board names and 3 pin hooks.**
   - **Risk**: the biggest one (saturation, trademark, seasonality), and how to sidestep it.
6. **Pick one.** Name the niche you would launch first, and say why in 2 sentences.
7. **Offer next steps.** For example: run `/incomeideas` on that niche, draft the product in Shopify or Gumroad, or design it in Canva. Never publish or post without the user's explicit yes.

## Rules
- Avoid trademarked phrases, characters and brand names in niche or product ideas.
- Don't invent sales or search numbers. Cite a source or label the figure as an estimate.
- Keep the output scannable: tables and short bullets, no filler.
