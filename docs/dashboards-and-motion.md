## 1. Dashboards: one live view of the business

Every number can be clicked to show the query behind it, and every chart shows when it was last refreshed.

**Connect first:** Shopify (floydcreativeshop.myshopify.com), Gumroad, Metricool (Instagram/TikTok/Pinterest), and CJdropshipping order data where available.

**Starter prompts**
- "Build a dashboard of this month's revenue by channel: Shopify, Gumroad, Etsy."
- "Show my top 10 affirmation products by units sold over the last 30 days, with week-over-week change."
- "Compare this week's email signups with last month's."
- "Dashboard of Instagram (floydessentials, floydcreativeshop), TikTok, and Pinterest reach vs. store sessions."
- "Flag CJdropshipping products with no sales in 30 days so I can decide to cut or refresh them."

**Weekly-review tie-in:** Pin the dashboard and use it as the data source for the 10-minute weekly review (revenue, best seller, worst performer, one decision).

**Hand-off options:** Amplitude, Grafana, Hex, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog, Sigma. Looker, Tableau, and monday.com are listed as coming soon.

## 2. Motion: short videos with no ads and no filming

Type `/motion` in the Claude message box. Claude writes code that animates **your own** text, charts, shapes, and images. It uses no generated footage and no AI people, and every word or timing can be edited. The result downloads as an MP4.

**Ready-to-use prompts for the product line**
1. **Affirmation card reel (15s):** "Animate 5 affirmations from our 'Anchored' card deck, one per 3 seconds, soft fade, brand colors, end card: floydcreativeshop.myshopify.com."
2. **Digital wallpaper reveal (10s):** "Show a phone mockup cycling through 4 affirmation wallpapers, with the text 'Instant download, $4'."
3. **Journal walkthrough (30s):** "Page-flip walkthrough of the 30-day affirmation journal: cover, daily prompt page, reflection page, price."
4. **Printable bundle value stack (20s):** "Animate the bundle contents stacking up, show the separate prices crossed out, then reveal the bundle price."
5. **Monthly wins explainer (30s):** "Turn this month's dashboard into a 30-second recap of top sellers and growth."

**Where to post:** Instagram Reels (floydessentials, floydcreativeshop), TikTok @floydcreativeshop, Pinterest Idea Pins, and Shopify and Gumroad product pages.

**Editing hand-offs:** Adobe, Descript, HeyGen, Higgsfield, invideo, Luma AI, Runway. The article lists Canva and Captions as coming soon.

> Motion requires a **Team or Enterprise** plan. On another plan, build the same storyboards with Slides/Design (available on all plans) and animate them in Canva.
