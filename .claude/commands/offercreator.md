# /offercreator

Turn a product into an offer people say yes to quickly. Build a clear promise, a stacked value, real bonuses, an honest guarantee and ethical urgency, then write the copy that sells it on organic channels only.

## Steps

1. **Pick the core product.** If none was named, pull products from Shopify and Gumroad, plus 90 days of sales and views, and suggest the 3 best candidates. Look for high views with low sales, or a product that is ready but unpublished. Ask the user to choose one. Then confirm:
   - what it is
   - who it's for (one specific buyer)
   - its current price and status
2. **Write the promise.** One sentence in the form: "[Specific result] in [timeframe] without [biggest pain or objection]." Give 3 versions, from safest to boldest, and flag the one you'd use. Keep it honest: no health, income or weight-loss claims that can't be backed up.
3. **Build the value stack.**
   - **Core product**: what's inside, with its own value.
   - **3–5 bonuses**, each removing an objection or speeding up the result. Examples: a quick-start guide, phone wallpapers, a printable tracker, a bonus affirmation deck, a private Pinterest board or Instagram broadcast channel. For each bonus, say whether it already exists or needs making, and how many hours it takes with Canva and Claude.
   - **Total**: the total "worth $X", and the offer price.
   - **Price tiers**: a good/better/best ladder (for example $9 / $19 / $37), with the middle tier as the intended pick, and the reason for each tier.
4. **Add risk reversal and urgency.**
   - **Guarantee**: one that's honest for digital goods, such as a "love it or we'll make it right" promise, or a free sample before buying.
   - **Urgency or scarcity**: real reasons only, such as a launch price ending on a set date, bonuses for the first 50 buyers, or a seasonal deadline. No fake countdown timers.
   - **Discount code**: a code name and its end date.
5. **Write the sales copy.**
   - **Sales page outline**: headline, subhead, problem, promise, what's inside, the value stack, bonuses, testimonials (placeholders labeled "add a real review" — never invented), guarantee, FAQ (5 questions), and the CTA.
   - **Listings**: a Shopify or Gumroad product description, and an Etsy version with its title and 13 tags.
   - **Objection replies**: 3 short enough to text, one of them genuinely funny.
   - **Email subject lines**: 5, each testing a different psychological hook, with the one you'd bet on.
6. **Plan an organic launch over 7 days.** For each day, give the channel, the content, the hook, and the CTA. Include:
   - Pinterest pins
   - Instagram and TikTok reels with a `/motion` prompt
   - a Facebook post
   - an email
   - a DM template for warm followers, under 4 sentences, that doesn't read as a pitch
7. **Offer next steps.** Wait for an explicit yes before each one:
   - update or create the product as a **draft** in Shopify or Gumroad
   - create the discount code
   - make the bonus files and mockups in Canva
   - schedule the launch in Metricool for review
   - add offer tracking to the channels dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/9EJnkcLTmK3NcFtxpm8K8E

## Rules
- Never publish, change live prices, create live discounts, post or email without the user's explicit approval.
- Keep urgency and claims truthful. No fake scarcity, invented testimonials, or health or income guarantees.
- Don't invent sales numbers. Every figure is either from a connector or labeled as an estimate.
- Keep the output scannable: tables and short bullets, no filler.
