# /productbundle

Design bundles that raise the average order value and give buyers an easy "yes". Group products the user already has, and add filler products only where a gap needs one. Use no paid ads and no inventory.

Theme or products from the user (may be empty): $ARGUMENTS

## Steps

1. **Take inventory.** Use whichever connectors are available, and note any that aren't connected:
   - **Shopify**: list products with price, type (digital or physical/CJdropshipping) and status. Pull the last 90 days of sales by product, and which products sell together if available.
   - **Gumroad**: list products (published or not, price, bundle status) and views and sales by product.

   Show a compact table of what exists.
2. **Find bundle logic.** Group products by:
   - **The buyer's goal**: "New Year reset", "self-care starter", "steady through the holidays".
   - **Format ladder**: printable + wallpaper + card deck on the same theme.
   - **Time**: a monthly planner set, or a 12-month bundle.
   - **Seasonal moments in the next 90 days**: Christmas gifts, New Year, Valentine's Day.

   Flag any gap where one small new item would complete a bundle. Say what it is and how long it takes to make with Canva and Claude.
3. **Propose 5 bundles.** For each one, give:
   - **Name and promise**: a name and a one-line promise to the buyer.
   - **Contents**: every item and its separate price.
   - **Price**: the "worth $X" total, the bundle price, and the savings as a percentage. Aim for 30–50% off the separate prices, ending in .99 or .00, with the reasoning.
   - **Store**: the best one for it (Shopify, Gumroad, or Etsy as a digital download).
   - **Fit**: whether it's a good upsell or cross-sell, and to which product.
   - **Effort**: hours to launch.
   - **Income estimate**: a conservative monthly range, with the math shown (traffic × conversion × price), labeled as an estimate.
4. **Rank them and pick one.** Rank by income ÷ effort and recommend the #1 bundle to launch this week.
5. **Build the launch kit for #1:**
   - **Shopify and Gumroad listing**: an SEO title, a 3-line value proposition, a "what's inside" list, who it's for, delivery and file formats, and an FAQ.
   - **Etsy**: a title (under 140 characters) and 13 tags.
   - **Mockup brief for Canva**: a flat-lay of every item, and a cover reading "X products, 1 price".
   - **Order-bump and upsell copy**: one line for the product page and one for the thank-you page.
   - **Promotion**: 3 Pinterest pins, 2 Instagram/TikTok captions with hashtags, and one `/motion` prompt for a 15-second reel stacking the items and revealing the price.
   - **Discount**: a launch code idea, with an end date.
6. **Offer next steps.** Wait for an explicit yes before each one:
   - create the bundle as a **draft** (a Gumroad bundle, or a Shopify product or collection)
   - make the mockup in Canva
   - schedule posts in Metricool
   - add the bundle to the channels dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/9EJnkcLTmK3NcFtxpm8K8E

## Rules
- Never publish, change live prices, post or email without the user's explicit approval. Create drafts only.
- Don't mix physical CJdropshipping items into digital-only bundles unless the user asks for it. Call out the shipping and fulfillment differences if they do.
- Don't invent sales numbers. Every figure is either from a connector or labeled as an estimate.
