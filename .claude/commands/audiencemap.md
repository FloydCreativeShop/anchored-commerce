# /audiencemap

Build a clear map of the user's audiences: who they are, what they want, where they spend time, and what makes them buy. Ground it in the user's own data first and outside research second. End with concrete product and content moves for each segment.

Brand or channel focus from the user (may be empty): $ARGUMENTS

## Steps

1. **Gather the evidence.** Use whichever connectors are available, and note any that aren't connected:
   - **Shopify**: customers and orders (count, repeat rate, country, device), sessions by referrer and device, and top products and landing pages for the last 90 days.
   - **Gumroad**: customers, sales by country, and views by referrer.
   - **Metricool**: by network, follower counts and growth, reach, top posts by saves, shares and outbound clicks, demographics (age, gender, country) where available, and best times to post.

   Report personal data only in aggregate. Never list individual customers' names or emails.
2. **Add outside research.** Use web search and check the dates. Look at:
   - how the audiences around the user's niches (affirmations, large-print planners, fitness and wellness for women 35+, self-care) talk about their problems: Reddit threads, Etsy reviews of similar products, Pinterest searches
   - the phrases and objections they repeat

   Cite sources.
3. **Define 3–5 audience segments.** For each one, give:
   - **Name and snapshot**: a short name and a one-line snapshot, such as "Busy caregiver, 45–60, wants 5 minutes of calm".
   - **Evidence**: which data points or sources point to this segment.
   - **Pains and desires**: the top 3 pains and the top 3 desired outcomes, in their own words.
   - **Buying moment**: what triggers a purchase (New Year, a diagnosis, a new job, a gift).
   - **Where they are**: the networks, the content formats they engage with, and the times.
   - **Spend**: price sensitivity and the likely price range.
   - **Objections**: the top 2, each with a one-line answer.
   - **Brand fit**: which brand serves them best, and which existing products fit.
4. **Map channels to segments.** Make a table: segments down the side, Shopify, Gumroad, Etsy, Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook across the top. Mark each cell strong, some or none, based on the data. Note the biggest mismatch, such as a channel with big reach that sends no store visits.
5. **Give moves for each segment.**
   - **Products**: 1 product to make and 1 to promote, with prices.
   - **Content**: 3 content angles with a sample hook for each.
   - **Lead magnet**: 1 free idea to collect emails.
   - **DM or comment reply**: 1 template that doesn't sound like a pitch, under 4 sentences.
6. **Focus.** Name the one segment to focus on for the next 30 days, and why, in 2 sentences. Then list 3 actions to take this week.
7. **Offer next steps.** For example: save this map as a doc, add an audience section to the channels dashboard (https://claude.ai/artifact/9EJnkcLTmK3NcFtxpm8K8E), run `/nichefinder` or `/digitalproduct` for the focus segment, or draft posts in Metricool. Never post, email or publish without the user's explicit yes.

## Rules
- Use only aggregated customer data, with no individual names, emails or addresses.
- Don't invent numbers or demographics. Every figure is either from a connector, cited from a source, or labeled as an assumption.
- Keep the output scannable: tables and short bullets, no filler.
